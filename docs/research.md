# Competitor research

Date: 2026-11-12. Purpose: decide what Ledgerly copies, what it avoids, and where the gap is for South African small businesses.

**How to read this.** "Does best" and "complaints" combine (a) web searches run on the date above and (b) general product knowledge. Most public comparison pages are affiliate or competitor-authored, so individual numbers are directional. Claims backed by a search result carry a source link; the rest are general knowledge and marked *(GK)*. Prices are in USD as quoted by the source and must be re-checked before being used in any marketing copy.

## 1. Product by product

### Odoo — modular ERP
- **Does best:** One database across CRM, invoicing, inventory, manufacturing, HR; install only the apps you need. Strongest of the group for stock/manufacturing/accounting depth ([ecosire](https://ecosire.com/hi/tools/erp-vs-erp/odoo-vs-zoho)). Rated 4.2 on 1,304 Capterra reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/135618-166175/Odoo-vs-Zoho-One)).
- **Complaints:** Needs an implementation partner for anything beyond defaults; version upgrades break customisations; UI is dense and desktop-first; per-user pricing adds up *(GK)*.
- **Take:** Copy the "modules you switch on" idea. Avoid needing a consultant to get started.

### Zoho One — suite of 45+ apps
- **Does best:** Enormous breadth for one per-employee price; good value for CRM and marketing in small teams ([ecosire](https://ecosire.com/hi/tools/erp-vs-erp/odoo-vs-zoho)). Rated 4.1 on 133 Capterra reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/135618-166175/Odoo-vs-Zoho-One)).
- **Complaints:** Feels like many separate apps with different UIs and separate settings; set-up effort is high; support quality varies *(GK)*.
- **Take:** One app, one navigation, one design language. Breadth must not show up as fragmentation.

### HoneyBook — clientflow for creatives
- **Does best:** Inquiry → proposal → contract → invoice → payment as one guided, good-looking flow. 4.7 on 680 Capterra reviews, overwhelmingly positive ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/162588-238825/HoneyBook-vs-Bonsai)).
- **Complaints:** Suits visual freelancers more than developers or writers ([freelancinghacks](https://freelancinghacks.com/honeybook-review)); set-up complexity and limits on advanced features are common review themes ([spotsaas](https://spotsaas.com/compare/honeybook-vs-bonsai)); payments are US/Canada-only, so not usable in SA *(GK)*.
- **Take:** Copy the single-document "smart file" flow: quote, sign and pay on one public page.

### Bonsai — freelancer suite
- **Does best:** Contract templates, proposals, time tracking → invoice, simple tax helpers. 4.6 on Capterra, 4.3 on G2 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/162588-238825/Honeybook-vs-Bonsai), [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/bonsai-vs-honeybook)).
- **Complaints:** Per-seat pricing multiplies for teams of 2–5 where flat-priced rivals stay level ([beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/10/22/honeybook-vs-dubsado-vs-bonsai-freelancer-client-management-guide)); acquired by Zoom in December 2025, which creates roadmap uncertainty ([same source](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/10/22/honeybook-vs-dubsado-vs-bonsai-freelancer-client-management-guide)).
- **Take:** Copy billable time → invoice in one tap and the template library. Avoid per-seat pricing for tiny teams.

### QuickBooks Online — accounting
- **Does best:** Deepest accounting feature set, biggest accountant ecosystem, strong bank feeds and reporting *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** The loudest complaints in the category are rising prices and poor support ([beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/04/05/quickbooks-alternatives-best-accounting-software-for-small-business)); frequent upselling ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/quickbooks-alternatives-signs)); sources cite a 21% rise in 2025 (unverified) ([costbench](https://costbench.com/compare/quickbooks-online-vs-xero/)). QuickBooks has also withdrawn from direct sales in South Africa in the past, which left local users migrating *(GK — verify before quoting)*.
- **Take:** Never put accounting jargon in front of an owner. Keep pricing flat and predictable.

### Xero — accounting
- **Does best:** Clean UI, unlimited users on every plan, strong SA bank feeds and accountant adoption *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Repeated price rises; entry plan is tightly capped; invoice customisation and payables weaker than QuickBooks ([layer3labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/comparisons/xero-alternatives-for-small-business), [costbench](https://costbench.com/compare/quickbooks-online-vs-xero/)).
- **Take:** Unlimited users is a loved feature. We are not a ledger; export cleanly to Xero/accountant rather than compete on double-entry.

### Wave — free invoicing and accounting
- **Does best:** Genuinely free starter tier with unlimited invoices and bookkeeping; ideal for a one-person business ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/quickbooks-alternatives)).
- **Complaints:** Outgrown quickly once there is staff or stock; payments and bank connections are North America-centric, so SA users get invoicing only *(GK)*.
- **Take:** A free tier that sends real invoices is the best acquisition channel in this category.

### monday.com — work management
- **Does best:** Colourful, flexible boards; quick to make a workflow look good; many views of the same data *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Three-seat minimum, features behind higher tiers, automation caps (250 actions/month on Standard per G2 reviews), learning curve ([jibble](https://www.jibble.io/reviews/monday-com/5-ways-monday-com-sucks), [smartsuite](https://smartsuite.com/blog/monday-review)).
- **Take:** Copy board/list/calendar views over one task table. Avoid seat minimums and metered automations.

### Notion — docs and databases
- **Does best:** Total flexibility; pages, databases and templates; pleasant writing experience *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Blank-page problem — you must build your own system; no time tracking or resource management, limited automation ([ClickUp](https://clickup.com/blog/notion-scaling-limitations/) — a competitor source).
- **Take:** Opinionated defaults beat a blank canvas for busy owners. This is the argument for onboarding that pre-configures the workspace.

### HubSpot — CRM
- **Does best:** Free CRM that is easy to start; pipeline, email tracking and timeline are best-in-class *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Pricing is among the top complaints on G2; steep jumps between tiers; limited support on Free/Starter ([Nutshell](https://www.nutshell.com/nl/nutshell-vs-hubspot-crm/problems-with-hubspot), [Teamgate](https://www.teamgate.com/blog/the-hubspot-trap/) — competitor sources).
- **Take:** Copy the contact timeline and kanban pipeline. Avoid cliff-edge pricing.

### Square — POS and payments
- **Does best:** Fastest path from nothing to taking a card payment; hardware plus software in one; simple flat fees *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Fund holds are the most common complaint, with 90-day holds on deactivated accounts and limited phone support ([cardpaymentoptions](https://cardpaymentoptions.com/credit-card-processors/square-market), [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/square-concern)). Not available in South Africa *(GK)*.
- **Take:** Yoco is the local Square. We integrate with it; we never hold funds ourselves.

### Shopify Admin — commerce back office
- **Does best:** Polished admin, excellent mobile app, command-style search, strong empty states and a set-up guide that drives activation *(GK)*.
- **Complaints:** Payout holds of 90–120 days reported in community threads ([Shopify community](https://community.shopify.com/t/are-shopify-payments-freezing-your-funds-too/114919/3)); the real cost comes from paid apps on top of the plan *(GK)*.
- **Take:** Copy the set-up guide and admin polish. Build the common needs in, so there is no app-store tax.

## 2. Patterns

**What the leaders share**
1. A guided first run that ends in a real outcome (first invoice, first sale), not a tour.
2. One timeline per customer showing everything that happened.
3. A public document page where the client can view, accept and pay.
4. Templates and sensible defaults instead of configuration.
5. A good mobile experience for the three daily jobs: check money, send invoice, log expense.

**What users complain about, across the board**
1. Price rises and per-seat pricing (QuickBooks, Xero, Bonsai, monday, HubSpot).
2. Complexity and set-up effort (Odoo, Zoho, Notion, monday).
3. Poor or absent support on cheap tiers (QuickBooks, HubSpot, Square).
4. Fragmentation — several tools or apps that do not share data (Zoho, Shopify apps).
5. Money held by the platform (Square, Shopify Payments).

## 3. The South African gap

- HoneyBook, Wave payments and Square do not process payments in SA. The polished "quote → sign → pay" tools are effectively unavailable here.
- Local payment rails are Yoco and PayFast, plus EFT, which remains the default for B2B invoices. A product that treats "paid by EFT, mark as paid, attach proof" as a first-class flow fits how SA businesses are actually paid.
- VAT at 15%, ZAR formatting, tax-invoice wording required by SARS, and POPIA are table stakes that global tools handle generically or not at all.
- WhatsApp is the dominant business messaging channel; "send invoice on WhatsApp" matters more than email polish.
- Connectivity and load-shedding make an offline-tolerant PWA a real feature, not a nicety.

## 4. Payment integration notes

- **Yoco:** Checkout API with hosted payment page. Webhooks follow the Standard Webhooks scheme: `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature` headers; signature is base64 HMAC-SHA256 over id, timestamp and raw body; secret is prefixed `whsec_` and shown once at subscription creation; 3-minute timestamp tolerance recommended ([Yoco docs](https://developer.yoco.com/online/api-reference/webhooks/verifying-events), [Hookdeck guide](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-yoco-webhooks-features-and-best-practices)).
- **PayFast:** Hosted redirect checkout with an ITN (instant transaction notification) callback. Verification as described by third-party libraries: MD5 signature over the posted fields plus passphrase, source-IP check, and a server-to-server validation call that must return `VALID` ([dj-payfast](https://dj-payfast.readthedocs.io/en/latest/webhooks.html)). **Not yet confirmed against PayFast's official docs — must be verified before Phase 3.**
- Both are hosted checkouts, so card data never touches our servers (PCI scope stays with the processor).
- Open design point for the spec: each workspace connects *its own* Yoco/PayFast merchant account so money goes directly to the business. Ledgerly never touches funds.

## 5. What this means for Ledgerly

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Onboarding provisions a working workspace from eight tapped answers | Fixes the complexity and blank-page complaints |
| Modules on/off per workspace, one navigation, one design system | Odoo's strength without Zoho's fragmentation |
| Public document page: view, accept/sign, pay by Yoco, PayFast or EFT | HoneyBook's flow, on SA rails |
| EFT and "mark as paid" as first-class | Matches real SA payment behaviour |
| WhatsApp share on every document | Local channel |
| Flat pricing, unlimited users, usable free tier | Direct answer to the top complaint category |
| Not a general ledger; clean exports for the accountant | Avoids competing with Xero where it is strongest |
| Merchant connects own processor; we never hold money | Avoids the fund-hold complaint class entirely |
| Offline-tolerant PWA | Local connectivity reality |
